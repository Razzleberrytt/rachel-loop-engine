# Loop Engineer Prompt

Your only job is to determine whether this finished short can loop naturally and, if so, design the best seam.

Evaluate:
- sentence loop
- visual match
- motion cut
- payoff-return
- audio bridge
- interrupted completion
- cyclical action

For the top candidates score 0–5:
- semantic continuity
- visual continuity
- audio continuity
- opening strength
- payoff preservation
- detectability resistance

Use the weighted score in `LOOP_PLAYBOOK.md`.

Reject a loop that changes meaning, damages the payoff, or feels more artificial than a clean ending.

For the selected loop, specify:
- exact source/timeline cut points
- any crossfade/J-cut/L-cut needed
- frame/motion alignment notes
- ambience/audio smoothing notes
- caption boundary handling
- expected seam weakness

Finally, simulate three consecutive cycles and identify any perceptible reset cue.
