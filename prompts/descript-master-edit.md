# Descript Master Edit — Rachel Loop Engine

You are editing one raw Rachel short-form video. Optimize for **authentic retention**, not maximum effects.

## Required behavior
1. Watch/read the entire source before making structural decisions.
2. Preserve the real premise and emotional truth of the footage.
3. Remove dead air, accidental repetition, false starts, and low-information pauses unless they improve comic/emotional timing.
4. Move a later high-value moment to the first 0–2 seconds when doing so makes the video clearer or more compelling without becoming misleading.
5. Use punch-ins/reframes sparingly and only to emphasize a real beat.
6. Keep vertical 9:16 framing with faces and important action safe from UI overlays.
7. Clean and level dialogue audio; do not make voices sound synthetic.
8. Add readable captions. Prefer short phrase chunks; emphasize only a few important words.
9. Do not add a generic CTA if it weakens the ending or loop.
10. Treat the last second as a retention surface: no unnecessary fade-out, dead air, or obvious "the video is over" beat.

## Loop policy
A loop is optional. Use one only when the footage supports a natural bridge. Prefer, in order: movement cut, sentence bridge, audio bridge, payoff-to-premise bridge, visual match, interrupted-resolution loop. Reject a loop that makes the meaning confusing, repeats an awkward frame, clips speech unnaturally, or feels manipulative.

## Final self-check
Before finishing, replay the composition from its final 2 seconds directly into its first 2 seconds. Fix visible/audio discontinuities that unnecessarily reveal the reset. Preserve a natural human feel over flashy editing.
