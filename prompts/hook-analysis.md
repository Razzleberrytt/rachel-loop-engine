# Hook Analysis Prompt

Analyze the entire source before choosing the opening.

Find up to three candidate opening moments. A candidate may come from anywhere in the raw clip.

For each candidate report:
- source timestamp range
- exact reason it earns attention
- clarity score 0–5
- curiosity score 0–5
- emotional score 0–5
- visual activity score 0–5
- payoff connection score 0–5
- authenticity score 0–5
- risk of confusing/spoiling the viewer

Do not invent clickbait wording that is unsupported by the video.

Prefer the strongest authentic moment over a generic text hook.
